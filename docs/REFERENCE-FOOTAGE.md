# Reference footage — per-clip analysis

Written 2026-08-26 (iteration 2). Source clips live in `reference/video/` (gitignored);
frames at 2 fps are pre-extracted under `reference/frames/<clip>/f_NNN.jpg` with 4×3
contact sheets `contact_N.jpg`; the first clip's frames are
`reference/frames/IMG_7841/video_*.jpg` plus `landing_sequence_10fps.jpg`.
Read with the plan (`docs/RENDER-PLAN.md`) and the first analysis
(`docs/ANALYSIS-render-2026-08-26.md`).

**Two venues, one world.** `IMG_7841`, `IMG_3523`, `IMG_3520` and `IMG_3396` are the
winter forest venue — the game's world stays this venue: snow everywhere, mixed bare
deciduous / snow-loaded spruce forest, low ridgelines. `IMG_3765` and `IMG_3768` are a
spring/summer water-ramp-style training site (snow strip on dry sagebrush hills, a
glass-fronted start building, staff in shorts): the hills stay white in the game — those
clips are used only for what they show better: kicker geometry from the base, the in-run
from behind the athlete, takeoff arm sweep, and the start-area layout.

The competition kicker wrap in `IMG_3523`/`IMG_3520` carries federation marks (a red
panel with "CAN" and a maple leaf). **Excluded from the game** per ground rule 3 —
generic panels/colours only. Same for the roundel flag on the knoll pole: a plain flag.

## IMG_7841 — winter sunny venue, top-of-in-run + landing sequence

- **Camera**: hand-held from the top/side of the in-run looking down the venue (original
  `video_*.jpg`), plus a telephoto close-up sequence of one landing at 10 fps
  (`landing_sequence_10fps.jpg`).
- **Venue geometry**: tall kicker block with near-vertical sides, lip well above a
  coach's head, take-off sends the athlete near-vertical; flat hand-raked table (rake
  marks visible), rounded knoll, then a ≈35–38° landing into a bowl with snow banks both
  sides; blue B-net fencing along both landing edges; orange marker poles; flag on a pole
  at the knoll; scaffold judges' platform at the top; buildings/cars/lodge in the valley.
- **Environment**: dense mixed woodland — mostly bare birch/maple with dark spruce/fir,
  snow on the conifers and under them; low rolling forested ridgelines; deep blue sky
  with cumulus.
- **Lighting/colour**: low hard sun from camera-left (analysis: ≈30–35° elevation), long
  crisp shadows; the snow is the brightest surface in frame; shadows read cool/blue.
- **Athlete technique**: white suit with a royal-blue panel/stripe, dark helmet, bib.
  Layout flight: arms overhead by the ears, body one line, skis together; arms open wide
  only for landing.
- **Landing sequence (`landing_sequence_10fps.jpg`, 0.1 s steps)**: touchdown flat on the
  ~36° surface → deep knee compression over ~0.2 s, boots approaching the surface, skis
  stay planar → plume grows from the ski tails to roughly 0.5× athlete height by ~0.3 s →
  peak plume ≈1–1.5 m above the surface at ~0.4–0.5 s → the cloud trails the athlete down
  the slope for 1–2 s while they extend and ride out arms-wide; twin furrows visible in
  the chop behind. Skis never disappear into the surface — the sink is roughly to sole
  depth at the deepest compression step.

## IMG_3765 — spring venue, base of kicker looking up (3.7 s)

- **Camera**: low at the base of the kicker, portrait, looking straight up the take-off;
  the best geometry reference for the kicker block.
- **Venue geometry** (`f_001`–`f_003`): the kicker is a clean sculpted **white block**:
  vertical side walls ≈2.5–3 m tall (about 1.5 athlete-heights), the take-off face rising
  above them another ~1 m and curving through the top to a **near-vertical lip**; a thin
  darker edge line where the snow face meets the side wall (a slight overhang/edge lip).
  The athlete leaves almost straight up: `f_003` shows a full layout directly above the
  lip, 2–3 m up, body straight, **skis parallel and together**, arms reaching overhead, a
  small spray puff still at the lip. Sharp dark shadow of the athlete on the block face.
  Orange snow fence and a snowcat/groomer behind; crews standing beside the block.
- **Environment**: deep blue zenith sky, dry brown hills with green conifers beyond the
  white strip; harsh high sun (spring) with a strong lens-flare streak in `f_001`/`f_002`.
- **Lighting/colour**: sunlit faces of the block blow out to near-white; shaded faces are
  cool but still bright; the block reads as one white mass against sky and hill.
- **Athlete technique**: arms sweep up together into the take-off; layout with skis
  parallel through the whole ascent.

## IMG_3768 — spring venue, in-run from behind the athlete (6.0 s)

- **Camera**: elevated behind and above the athlete, following down the in-run — the
  "behind-the-athlete" game camera reference.
- **Venue geometry** (`f_002`, `f_011`): **two parallel prepared lanes** (dual in-runs)
  separated by one lane-width of rough snow, running from the flat start apron down
  through the transition to the table/kicker; the in-run is a clean white ribbon with
  faint corduroy and previous ski lines. At the top: a glass-fronted start building (full
  height windows, cantilevered roof), a scaffold judges' tower with people on it, and
  crews/tooling along the sides. The corridor of white is narrow — dry ground begins
  almost immediately outside the prepared lanes.
- **Environment/lighting**: hard high spring sun, short shadows, deep blue sky; the snow
  strip reads slightly grey against the warm hills but still the brightest surface.
- **Athlete technique** (`f_002` and the sheet): in the in-run the athlete is in a **low
  crouch** — back flat and near-horizontal, knees deeply bent, hands held **forward at
  knee height** (gloves ahead of the knees, forearms roughly horizontal), skis hip-width.
  Approaching the transition the arms begin to rise; at the lip **both arms sweep up
  together** (sheet frames 8–9: symmetric, elbows locked out overhead).

## IMG_3523 — winter venue, judges-tower view of the whole run, overcast (10.5 s)

- **Camera**: from the judges' platform at the top of the landing, high on the right
  side, looking down the full venue — landing bowl, knoll, table, kicker and in-run all
  in one frame with the forest walls behind.
- **Venue geometry** (`contact_1`, `f_017`, `contact_2`): the run is a **white corridor
  cut through forest**, blue B-net fencing continuously along **both** edges from the
  table through the landing and curving around the bottom of the bowl; the fence line
  sags slightly between posts. Red competition flags hang on the fence (excluded marks).
  Orange/red wands mark the landing line-up. The landing surface is **chopped**: loose
  broken snow with dense ski tracks, twin furrows from every landing, larger broken
  blocks/clods pushed to the sides (some slightly green-tinged ice chunks), a smoother
  raked band down the middle. The knoll carries a flag pole with a blue flag
  (`f_017`). The kicker block is wrapped in red/white federation panels (excluded).
  A timber-deck platform on dark containers stands beside the kicker (judges'/start
  deck), and a container stack sits at the top of the run.
- **Environment**: overcast — flat white-grey sky, **no visible shadows**; bare
  deciduous trees with snow-loaded dark spruce behind them; snow-covered roofs of houses
  in the background valley (behind the forest).
- **Lighting/colour**: everything lit by soft sky light; the snow corridor reads
  mid-bright, the fence/net blue saturates, athlete suits (blue, yellow) are the most
  saturated elements in frame. The sky is *lighter* than the snow in places (flat light)
  — unlike the sunny clips where the snow is brightest.
- **Athlete technique** (`f_017` and the sheet): blue-suit athlete in a straight layout
  rotation just above the knoll, arms reaching, skis parallel; on touchdown a plume
  rises, then the ride-out with arms wide.

## IMG_3520 — winter venue, high side view above the landing, overcast (7.9 s)

- **Camera**: from the judges'/start deck looking down at the landing bowl from the

- **Venue geometry** (`contact_1`, `contact_2`, `f_006`): confirms `IMG_3523`: continuous

  marking the landing centreline, spaced regularly down-slope; blue flag on a pole at the
  knoll crest; the timber deck on dark shipping containers (the judges' platform) above
  the kicker with people on it; officials/coaches in **red jackets** standing at the
  fence at the knoll. Chopped landing with side clods; roll-out groomed and smoother.
- **Environment**: same bare-deciduous + snow-loaded spruce forest, houses with snowy
  roofs behind, low forested ridge on the horizon, overcast.
- **Lighting/colour**: flat, slightly cooler than `IMG_3523`; the red wands/jackets are
  strong colour accents against an almost monochrome white/grey/brown scene.
- **Athlete technique**: yellow-bib athlete, layout with arms overhead, takeoff arm sweep
  symmetric; landing plume and ride-out as in `IMG_7841`.

## IMG_3396 — night training session under floodlights in heavy falling snow (4.7 s)

The reference for plan item **P9 (night snowfall mode)**.

- **Camera**: from the out-run/base area near coaches, looking up the lit corridor:
  landing, table and kicker against a black sky; later frames track the athlete leaving
  the kicker.
- **Venue geometry** (`f_001`–`f_005`): only the lit corridor exists visually — the
  run, its banks and the kicker emerge from darkness; **no horizon detail, no sky
  gradient**: near-black sky. The kicker block (`f_005`) reads **near-pure-white** and
  dominates the frame, its take-off face and curved lip crisp against the black.
- **Lighting** (`f_001`–`f_004`): **5–7 warm floodlights** on poles along the in-run
  crest and the table — halated yellow-white discs, colour ~2800–3500 K against the
  cold snow. Light pools on the snow with fast falloff: surfaces inside a pool read
  bright white with visible texture (chopped table, corduroy landing); between and
  beyond pools the snow drops quickly through grey to black. Snow reflectance stays
  high — lit snow is white, not grey. A few far lights pin the top of the run.
- **Falling snow** (all frames): dense. Against the black sky flakes appear as short
  **elongated white streaks** (motion blur at 1/30–1/60 s), roughly 4–15 px long in the
  portrait frames, thicker/brighter near the floodlights, fainter and shorter against
  dark ground. Density: dozens of visible streaks per frame near the lights, sparse
  individually but continuous overall — a steady heavy snowfall. Drift is slight.
- **Takeoff puff and landing cloud** (`f_006`–`f_008`): as the athlete leaves the lip a
  **puff of snow lifts off behind them** — a plume of loose snow with visible chunks
  rising ~0.5–1 m off the lip and drifting up-slope-back into the light, dissipating in
  ~1 s. The landing throws a **much larger, billowing cloud** ~1.5–2 m tall that lingers
  ~1.5–2 s, backlit so it glows. Athlete in a white suit reads as a silhouette where the
  sky is black and blows out when crossing a light pool.
- **People/props**: coaches under the lights in the foreground (dark jackets, one with a
  clipboard), orange wands at the pool edges, a shovel/tooling left by the table.

## Deltas vs the game

Concrete, measurable changes, each mapped to a plan priority (appended to
`docs/RENDER-PLAN.md` under the matching priority):

1. **P1 — Kicker block shape**: vertical side walls ≈2.5–3 m with the take-off face
   curving to a near-vertical lip above them (IMG_3765 f_001–f_003); a thin dark edge
   line at the wall/face junction; the block reads as one white mass. Today's kicker is
   stacked rotated boxes — rebuild as a sculpted block in the P1 continuous surface.
2. **P1 — In-run lanes**: two parallel prepared lanes separated by rough snow, faint
   corduroy, previous ski lines; the white corridor narrow against the surroundings
   (IMG_3768 f_002, f_011). Visual only; physics keeps the single profile.
3. **P2 — Sunny sky**: deep blue zenith with cumulus, sun ≈30–35° from camera-left, hard
   shadows, snow the brightest surface in frame (IMG_7841). Confirms the plan; nothing
   new beyond it.
4. **P3 — Fencing**: continuous blue B-net along **both** edges from table to run-out,
   sagging slightly between posts, curving around the landing bowl (IMG_3523 contact_1,
   IMG_3520 f_006). Instanced posts + alpha-cut strips per the plan.
5. **P3 — Landing wands**: two rows of orange/red wands with small pennants marking the
   landing centreline, regularly spaced down-slope (IMG_3520 f_006). New detail.
6. **P3 — Knoll flag**: flag on a pole at the knoll crest (IMG_3523 f_017, IMG_3520) —
   plain blue flag, no roundel.
7. **P3 — Judges' structure**: timber-deck platform on dark shipping containers beside
   the kicker/at the top (IMG_3523, IMG_3520 f_006); plus the plan's tower/scaffold.
8. **P3 — People**: 2–3 static coach figures in **red jackets** at the knoll fence
   (IMG_3520, IMG_7841) — red as the accent colour against the white/blue scene.
9. **P4 — Forest mix**: bare deciduous majority, snow-loaded dark spruce minority,
   walls closing in on both sides of the landing, houses with snowy roofs in the valley
   behind the trees (IMG_3523/IMG_3520 backgrounds).
10. **P5 — Surfaces**: corduroy in-run with two grooved lanes (delta 2); hand-raked
    table/knoll (rake marks); **chopped landing**: dense ski-track furrows and twin
    landing furrows, loose chunks/clods 10–30 cm pushed to the sides (subtle green-tinged
    ice in places), smoother raked centre band (IMG_3523 f_017, IMG_3520 contact_2) —
    feeds `blender/bake_landing.py`.
11. **P5 — Landing spray**: plume from the ski tails at touchdown, ~1–1.5 m above the
    surface at peak (~0.4–0.5 s after touchdown), trailing the athlete 1–2 s; sink to
    roughly sole depth at deepest compression, skis never swallowed (IMG_7841
    landing_sequence steps 1–7). Matches the contact rules already pinned in the
    progress file; scale `_spawnSnowBurst` to this.
12. **P6 — Horizon**: low forested ridgelines; a few snowy roofs at the valley edge
    behind the forest; no cone peaks.
13. **P7 — In-run stance**: low crouch, back near-horizontal, hands forward at knee
    height, arms quiet until the transition (IMG_3768 f_002); both arms sweep up
    together, elbows locked, finishing overhead by the ears (IMG_3768 sheet 8–9);
    layout flight with skis parallel and together (IMG_3765 f_003); arms open wide for
    the landing ride-out (IMG_7841 sequence end).
14. **P9 — Night mode** (from IMG_3396): 5–7 warm (~2800–3500 K) floodlights on poles
    along the in-run crest and table, halated; near-black sky with **no horizon
    detail**; kicker/table reading near-pure-white inside pools; fast falloff to black
    between pools (pool-edge falloff, not uniform fog); lit snow stays white;
    falling snow as elongated streaks 4–15 px, denser/brighter near lights; takeoff
    puff lifting off the lip (~0.5–1 m, ~1 s) and a bigger, slower landing cloud
    (~1.5–2 m, ~2 s).
