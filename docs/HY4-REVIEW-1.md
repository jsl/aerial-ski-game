# HY4 adversarial review 1

1. **blocker — P13 does not reconcile the pose and the no-tearing invariant is still broken.** Evidence: `game.js:682-698` calls `AerialEngine.kinematics.reconcile(meshes)` and `checkTearing(meshes)` *before* writing `computePose()`'s transforms, so the next loop overwrites the reconciliation. `engine/core/kinematics.js:7-17,32-38` also uses one `h` for both sides of each joint (including `0.30` for the `0.25 m` lower arm and `0.36` for a ski) rather than the two real segment lengths. Its alleged tear gate only rejects a driver-centre distance greater than `3*h` (0.90–1.08 m); it never measures a skinned triangle. Independent Playwright instrumentation in `~/aerial-agents/scratch/hy4-review/probe.js` measured ready hip gaps of 0.1067/0.0754 m, crouch hip gaps of 0.2043/0.1588 m, flight maxima of 0.2082 m neck / 0.3083 m elbow / 0.5413 m hip, and a maximum deformed-edge/rest-edge ratio of **30.2901x** (crouch: 19.3740x). The baseline worst ratio was also 30.2901x, so the claimed P13 closure did not improve the worst case. There is no kinematics test; the only test-tree references load the module. Acceptance: for every sampled non-crash frame from ready stance through crouch, takeoff, flight and landing, every adjacent driver endpoint is ≤0.02 m apart and every deformed skinned-triangle edge is <3x its rest length; enforce both against the real Babylon/skinned mesh with a mutation that proves each gate fails when the solver is bypassed, and visually show unshredded crouch and flight shots.

3. **major — the new arm gate measures the elbow as the hand, and even that proxy fails the required drive run.** Evidence: the shipped chain explicitly uses local `+Y` toward the wrist (`game.js:726-729,750-751,800-820`), but `_bootSkiProbe()` calls local `-Y` the hand (`game.js:2459-2469`), which is the elbow endpoint in `_inrunArmForward`'s construction. `tests/drive/inrun-contact.spec.js:69-77` therefore checks the wrong point. The dedicated and isolated drive runs both fail this proxy at **-0.2335 m** in gate crouch and **-0.2205 m** mid-inrun (required -0.40…-0.25 m). Independently measuring the actual `+Y` wrist endpoint at full gate crouch gives hand-z minus hip-z **-0.6194 m (L) / -0.5935 m (R)**, outside the band in the opposite direction. Acceptance: measure the actual wrist/glove endpoint, not the elbow; both hands must remain -0.40…-0.25 m ahead of their hips and below their shoulders at gate crouch and throughout the mid-inrun hold, with a known-bad endpoint/sign mutation rejected.
