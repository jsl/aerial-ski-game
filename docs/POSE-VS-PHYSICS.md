# Pose vs physics — reference-footage analysis (lane D)

Generated by `node tools/pose_vs_physics.js` from `reference/pose/<clip>/keypoints.json`
(RTMPose COCO-17 at 30 fps, all detected people per frame). Per-frame chosen track in
`reference/pose/<clip>/athlete_track.json` (local, gitignored); review sheets in
`reference/pose/<clip>/overlay_sheet_track.jpg`. Do not edit the tables by hand.

## Method — athlete track (iteration 1)

Per clip, all detected people are tracked by continuity (`tools/pose/track.js`):

- a keypoint with score < 0.5 is missing. A person is measured from its valid
  keypoints: hip and shoulder midpoints give a torso size (shoulder-to-hip distance
  in video px); when hips or shoulders are missing the anchor falls back to knees,
  elbows or the valid-keypoint bounding-box centre and the size to 0.5 x bbox height
  (flagged `sizeSource: "bbox"` in the track file, coarser but keeps the track alive
  through low-score stretches);
- the tracked person is the usable person whose hip midpoint is nearest to the previous
  tracked hips, gated to a plausible step (distance <= max(3.5 torso, 12% of frame
  diagonal)) and a plausible size change per frame (ratio in [0.4, 2.5]); cost =
  distance/torso + 0.5·|log size ratio|; after 5 consecutive lost frames the gates
  widen (ratio [0.2, 5]) so an athlete re-entering far away and small (e.g. airborne)
  can be re-acquired;
- the seed is not assumed to be in frame 0: the tracker is re-run from every usable
  person in every ~15th frame and the run with the best score wins, where score =
  total hip travel in px (the athlete crosses the frame; bystanders stay put)
  + 250·coverage + 50·mean confidence;
- gaps of <= 3 frames are linearly interpolated and flagged `interpolated`; longer gaps
  stay missing and tracking resumes against the last known state;
- hip/shoulder midpoints and torso size are smoothed with a 3-tap median.

## Scale assumption and uncertainty

All numbers are camera-plane 2D (720x1280 video px). Metre scale is only meaningful with
an assumption; we use a ~1.7 m athlete with a 1.55 m ski (per `docs/LANE-D.md`). For pixel
scale we use the torso: shoulder-midpoint to hip-midpoint ≈ 0.50 m on a 1.7 m athlete,
±15% for build and posture. Torso size in px varies with distance to the camera and crop,
so each clip quotes the median and the 5–95% band; the implied px/m is the band, not a
constant. Perspective foreshortening of the torso (leans toward/away from camera) is the
dominant uncertainty for any metre conversion, typically ±20%.

## Rotation metrics from the tracked pose (iteration 2)

Method (`tools/pose/metrics.js`): the shoulder-midpoint → hip-midpoint vector angle θ(f)
(image plane, 0° = upright) is unwrapped across each clip (unwrap resets after gaps > 3
frames so cuts never fake rotation) and differentiated to ω in °/s, 3-tap-median smoothed.
Rotation intervals are maximal runs with |ω| ≥ 90°/s (runs ≤ 2 frames apart merged, never
across gaps); a flips estimate is only reported for intervals that plausibly contain whole
flips (≥ 0.25 s, ≥ 180° captured turn, peak ≤ 3000°/s). Per-frame θ/ω series and interval
details are in `reference/pose/<clip>/athlete_metrics.json` (local).

## Kinematics (iteration 3) — arm sweep into the lip, and what the geometry rules out

Method (`tools/pose/kine.js`): the tracked person's full COCO-17 keypoints are re-joined
per frame via the track's `person` index (the track file itself stores only hip/shoulder
midpoints). All metrics are interior joint angles (deg, image plane) or distances
normalised by the torso length, so no px/m scale assumption enters them. Wrist height
relative to the shoulders (wr/sh, torso units; negative = below the shoulders) is the
arm-sweep signal; the sweep detector looks for a sustained ≥ 0.6-torso rise over 8 frames
that keeps rising. Per-frame series and windows: `reference/pose/<clip>/athlete_kine.json`
(local).

## Tracked-crop re-extraction with test-time rotation (reviewer fix, iteration 3)

The reviewer requested a rotation-aware re-extraction: `tools/pose/reextract_tracked.py`
(venv python) re-runs RTMPose on a per-frame crop centred on the tracked hip (side ≈ 3×
the person height, upscaled so the person is ≥ 600 px tall), at 0°/90°/180°/270°, keeping
every detected person with keypoints rotated back to video px (`keypoints_tracked.json`,
local). The same tracker was re-run on that file; frames are decoded via ffmpeg with the
same filter chain as the original extraction (cv2 alone mis-applies rotation/colour:
best-person mean score 0.19 vs 0.64 re-extracted on IMG_7841). Windows per
`RX_WINDOW` below; IMG_3396 skipped per the reviewer (night footage untrackable),
